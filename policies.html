Store Policies

The Fine Print, Kindly Put

Digital Delivery

Printable products are delivered instantly after purchase as PDF downloads. You'll find a download link on the confirmation page and in your email. Files are sized for US Letter paper and print beautifully on A4 with "fit to page."

Personal Use

Your purchase includes a personal-use licence. Print as many copies as you like for your own kitchen. Please don't resell, share or redistribute the files.

Refunds

Because digital files can't be returned, all sales of digital products are final. If a file is damaged, incomplete or won't open, write to us and we'll gladly send a fresh copy or make it right. Policies for physical merch and supplies will be posted when those items launch.

Allergens & Food Safety

Our recipes use common allergens, including wheat, milk, eggs, tree nuts (such as pecans). Please read every ingredient list carefully and substitute as needed. Recipes are shared for home use. Always follow safe food-handling practices, and bake to the temperatures given.

Candy and food items, when they arrive, will carry the manufacturer's own ingredient and allergen labelling.

Contact

Questions, kind words or download troubles? Email hello@example.com. We reply within two business days.
