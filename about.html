About

Our Story

Cakes and Pies Oh My began with a simple belief: the best bakes are the ones we share.

We create printable recipe collections, decorating guides and planners that bring a little elegance to everyday baking. Every recipe is a dependable classic, written clearly and measured in cups and grams. Every page is dressed in painted artwork you'll be glad to leave out on the counter.

Bakery merch and a carefully chosen selection of kitchen and baking supplies are coming soon. We hope our pages find a happy home beside your mixing bowls.
